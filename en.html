About us

We help children
reach their potential

Mel is a team of specialists who help children develop, learn new skills and become more independent.

We take time to understand each child and build an individual support plan based on modern, evidence-based approaches. We work with children with a range of developmental needs and help parents better understand what their child needs and how to support them at every stage. We are based in Kemerovo and help families find solutions that suit their child.

every session is
a small step forward

Learning difficulties

We help children overcome difficulties with reading, writing, maths and keeping up with the school curriculum.

Social difficulties

We develop communication skills, interaction with peers and adapting to a group.

Behavioural difficulties

We work with behavioural challenges and help children build self-regulation and self-control.

Speech development

Starting and developing speech, building phrases, correcting pronunciation and support for delayed speech development.

An individual approach

We take each child's development into account and build our work around their strengths, current needs and their own pace.

Support for children with special needs

We help children with autism (ASD), developmental delays, speech delays and ADHD develop speech, attention, thinking, communication and independence. Each programme is tailored to the child's needs and abilities.

Services

What we offer

We help children develop essential skills, overcome difficulties and discover their own abilities. Every child gets an individual programme based on their age, development, current needs and strengths. Our goal is not just to hold sessions, but to understand what the child needs, choose the right areas of work and set out a clear plan of support — so the child can steadily master new skills and make visible progress.

First step

Where the road begins

Here is how it works — from the first call to an individual plan — so you can feel at ease before we even meet.

the future
  1. 1

    A call

    Call us or message us on MAX. Tell us what worries you — we'll find the right specialist and a convenient time.

    Getting to know you

    The specialist observes your child at play and talks with you in detail. Bring reports from other specialists, if you have any.
